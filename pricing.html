Pricing questions

Why is it so much cheaper than an agent?

Because the expensive parts of letting — verification, paperwork, registration and payment collection — are automated. People step in where judgement is needed: verification exceptions, viewings, inspections and disputes.

Is the Trakheesi permit included?

Yes. List.me covers the advertising permit and QR code for that listing, issued through our brokerage licence under a Form A marketing agreement you sign in the app.

Do I pay if my property does not let?

You pay List.me when the advert goes live. Let.me is only charged when a tenant signs.

What does Collect.me actually cost?

1% of rent collected, capped at AED 150 a month per tenancy. Cheque custody alone is free.

Are there renewal fees?

Tenants pay only the government Ejari renewal fee. Landlords can renew themselves for free or buy the Renewal pack for AED 349.
