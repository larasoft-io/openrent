Rent and renewal

Rent increases capped by the Smart Rental Index

On renewal, rent can only rise if it is more than 10% below the index rent for your building: up to 5% (11–20% below), 10% (21–30%), 15% (31–40%) or 20% (more than 40% below).

For you: maximum next year is AED 110,250.

DXB-009 · Decree 43/2013
90 days’ notice to change any term

Either side wanting to change the rent or any other term at renewal must tell the other at least 90 days before the contract ends, unless both agreed a different period.

For you: the cut-off is Mon 30 Nov 2026.

DXB-014 · Law 26/2007 Art. 14 (as amended)
Automatic renewal on the same terms

If the term ends and the tenant stays on without objection, the tenancy renews for the same period (or one year, whichever is shorter) on the same terms.

DXB-006 · Law 26/2007 Art. 6
Payment terms are written into the contract

There is no mandatory number of cheques. Monthly, quarterly or half-yearly instalments are allowed when both sides agree, and must be stated in the contract. Rent is the same total whichever plan is chosen.

DXB-PAY · Unified tenancy contract

Your deposit

Security deposit is refundable

A landlord may take a deposit to cover damage, and must return it — or the balance — when the tenancy ends. The law sets no maximum; market custom is 5% (unfurnished) or 10% (furnished).

DXB-020 · Law 26/2007 Art. 20
Hand back in the same condition, fair wear and tear excepted

Tenants return the home as they received it, allowing for normal use. Deductions need evidence.

DXB-021 · Law 26/2007 Art. 21
Deposits capped, safeguarded and returned in 14 days

Deposits are limited to 5% of annual rent (10% furnished), held in a safeguarded client account rather than by the landlord, and returned within 14 days of check-out unless a deduction is evidenced.

For you: AED 5,250 is held under certificate DEP-2602-0311.

OR-101 · openrent.me standard — goes further than the law

Repairs and your home

Landlord keeps the home in good repair

Unless agreed otherwise, the landlord is responsible for maintenance and for fixing defects that affect the tenant’s use of the home.

DXB-016 · Law 26/2007 Art. 16
No cutting services or locking out

A landlord must not disconnect utilities or otherwise stop the tenant using the home. Disputes go to the Rental Disputes Center, not self-help.

DXB-034 · Law 26/2007 Art. 34
Small repairs threshold

Tenants cover minor fixes up to AED 500 per item (light bulbs, blocked drains they caused). Everything else is the landlord’s cost. The threshold can be lowered, not raised.

For you: your landlord lowered this to AED 250.

OR-103 · openrent.me standard
Emergency response within 4 hours

No cooling in summer, flooding, no power or a security risk are emergencies. If the landlord does not respond in 60 minutes, openrent.me dispatches an approved contractor up to a pre-agreed limit.

OR-104 · openrent.me standard
No alterations without consent

Tenants need the landlord’s permission (and any required permits) before making changes to the property.

DXB-019 · Law 26/2007 Art. 19

Staying and leaving

Eviction during the term — limited grounds, 30-day notice

Before expiry a landlord can only seek eviction on listed grounds — for example unpaid rent 30 days after a notice served through a notary public or registered mail, unauthorised subletting, illegal use or serious damage.

DXB-025A · Law 26/2007 Art. 25(1) (as amended)
Eviction at expiry — 12 months’ notarised notice

To recover a home at the end of the term (owner’s or first-degree relative’s own use, sale, demolition or major works) the landlord must give at least 12 months’ notice, stating the reason, through a notary public or registered mail.

DXB-025B · Law 26/2007 Art. 25(2) (as amended)
No re-letting after an own-use eviction

A home recovered for the owner’s own use cannot be let to someone else for 2 years (residential). If it is, the former tenant may claim compensation.

DXB-026 · Law 26/2007 Art. 26
A sale does not end the tenancy

If the property changes hands, the new owner takes over the existing tenancy on the same terms.

DXB-027 · Law 26/2007 Art. 28
A fair way out for tenants

Dubai law gives tenants no automatic right to leave early. Every openrent.me contract includes a break clause: 60 days’ notice and at most two months’ rent. Landlords can offer better, never worse.

For you: one month’s rent instead of two.

OR-102 · openrent.me standard — goes further than the law

Living arrangements

Everyone living in the home is registered

All occupants — family, flatmates, long-stay guests — should be recorded against the Ejari via Dubai REST.

DXB-OCC · DLD co-occupant registration (Dubai REST)
No subletting without written consent

A tenant cannot sublet or assign the tenancy unless the landlord agrees in writing.

DXB-024 · Law 26/2007 Art. 24
Shared housing needs a permit — tenants cannot sublet rooms or bed spaces

Letting rooms, partitions or bed spaces is only allowed by the owner or an authorised operator holding a shared-housing permit, within occupancy and safety standards. Fines can reach AED 1,000,000.

DXB-S04 · Law 4/2026 on shared housing (in force since late Aug 2026)

If things go wrong

Disputes go to the Rental Disputes Center

The RDC hears all landlord–tenant disputes. The filing fee is 3.5% of annual rent (minimum AED 500, maximum AED 20,000); half is refunded if you settle.

For you: the filing fee would be AED 3,675 (3.5% of AED 105,000).

DXB-RDC · Decree 26/2013 establishing the RDC
No agency commission for tenants

Tenants never pay a percentage commission. Fees are flat, shown before you commit, and government fees are passed on at cost.
