Design system

Empty, error and loading states

What every screen shows before there is data, while it loads, and when something fails. The same blocks are used by every role, so a blank list or a failed load always looks and reads the same.

How we write them

Say what happened, plainly

“We couldn’t load your payments”, not “Error 503”. No blame on the user.

Say what is safe

Rent, deposits and contracts are what people worry about. Tell them it is not affected.

Always offer a next step

Try again, a way round it, or the first thing to do when a list is empty.

Give a reference

Every error shows a code to quote to support, and whether other services are up.

Skeletons match the page

Loading shows the shape of what is coming, so nothing jumps when it arrives.

Keep working offline

Saved pages stay readable; actions queue on the device and send on reconnect.
