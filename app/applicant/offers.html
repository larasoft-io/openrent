How offers work

You can have up to 3 open offers. Each lasts 72 hours unless the owner answers sooner.

When one is accepted, the others are withdrawn for you. You then have 48 hours to pass referencing and sign.

There is no holding fee and no commission. You only pay once you sign: the deposit, the Move.me fee and the Ejari government fee.
