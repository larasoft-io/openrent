What changes

OR-101Our standardParameter
Change 1 of 3

Deposits returned faster

Live · 2026.3Deposits are returned within 14 days of check-out unless a deduction is evidenced.
Draft · 2027.1Deposits are returned within 10 days of check-out unless a deduction is evidenced.
Applies to

Better for tenants and does not alter anything a landlord agreed to pay, so it can protect every existing tenancy from the effective date.

OR-104Our standardParameter
Change 2 of 3

Higher emergency dispatch limit

Live · 2026.3If the landlord has not responded in 60 minutes, an approved contractor is dispatched up to AED 1,500.
Draft · 2027.1If the landlord has not responded in 60 minutes, an approved contractor is dispatched up to AED 2,000.
Applies to

This raises what a landlord may be charged without approval, so it applies to new contracts only; existing landlords are invited to opt in.

DXB-OCCProcessEnforcement
Change 3 of 3

Occupant check every six months

Live · 2026.3Tenants are prompted to confirm occupants at move-in and when they tell us the household changed.
Draft · 2027.1Tenants are prompted to confirm occupants at move-in and every six months.
Applies to

The underlying requirement is unchanged; only our reminder cadence moves. Safe for all tenancies.

What “applies to” means

All existing tenancies
Takes effect for every active tenancy on the effective date. Use for legal changes that bind everyone, and for protections that only improve a tenant’s position.
New tenancies only
Contracts signed on or after the effective date. Existing tenancies keep the version they signed under — used when a change alters cost or risk that was priced into a contract.
From next renewal
Each tenancy moves across when it renews, so the whole book migrates within twelve months without reopening live contracts.
