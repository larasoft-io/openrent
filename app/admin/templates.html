Why some wording is locked

A notice with the wrong ground or period can be thrown out at the Rental Disputes Center. Locked documents can only change through a ruleset draft approved by legal counsel.

Early exit (break clause)
OR-102 · Fair way out

Tenant may leave with 60 days’ notice and a payment of up to two months’ rent.

How clauses become guardrails

Each clause has parameters with a limit taken from a rule. In the contract builder a landlord can move a slider towards the tenant’s favour, never past the limit.
