What happens when something is down

Ejari unavailable
Contracts can still be signed; registration queues and the Ejari desk is alerted. Rent is not released until the certificate arrives.
DXB-001 · Ejari
No index data for a unit
The rent-increase check goes to a manager with two-person approval — never “allow by default”.
DXB-009 · Rent cap
Permit service unavailable
Listings stay in “ready” and go live automatically once a permit is issued.
DXB-002 · Advertising permit
Direct debit file rejected
Tenants are told, nothing is marked late, and the file is re-presented the next banking day.
