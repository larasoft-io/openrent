Governance

Roles and permissions

Five core roles run a tenancy. Ten supporting roles exist because real tenancies involve more people than a landlord and a tenant — each gets the least access that lets them do their part.

Core roles

Live
Tenant
5,410 accounts

Rents one home. Pays, reports repairs, renews, moves out.

Own tenancy only

Live
Landlord
1,920 accounts

Owns one or many properties at different stages of the letting cycle.

Own properties only

Live
Contractor
186 companies

Quotes for and completes maintenance, cleaning and inspections.

Assigned jobs only

Live
OpenRent Manager
14 staff

RERA-carded broker who verifies, registers, mediates and decides.

Assigned portfolio

Live
Super Admin
3 staff

Runs the platform: rulesets, prices, roles and integrations.

Platform-wide · no client-money actions alone

Supporting roles

Live
Owner’s Representative
64 people acting for 211 owners

Many Dubai owners live abroad. A family member or property manager acts for them under a notarised power of attorney.

Exactly what the PoA allows, until it expires

Live
Co-tenant / Occupant
3,870 registered

Partners, children and flatmates live in the home and must be on the Ejari, but did not sign the contract.

Can report repairs and see house information; cannot sign or pay

Beta
Guarantor / Corporate tenant
142 employers

Companies often lease homes for staff. HR pays and signs; the employee lives there.

Pays and signs; cannot see the occupant’s messages

Live
Field Agent
9 staff

Hosts viewings, hands over keys, and photographs move-in and check-out condition.

Today’s appointments and their properties

Live
Compliance Officer (MLRO)
2 staff

A licensed brokerage must know its customers and report suspicious activity.

Identity documents and alerts; no pricing or product access

Live
Finance Officer
4 staff

Reconciles client accounts and is the second approver on every payout.

Ledgers and payouts; cannot edit tenancies

Live
Support Agent
7 staff

First line for questions in Arabic, English, Hindi and Urdu.

Read-only view of an account, with a reason

Beta
Building / Community Manager
58 partner communities

Issues move-in and move-out permits and NOCs, and announces building works.

Their building’s units and permit requests only

Live
Legal Counsel
1 external firm

Reviews every ruleset change against the legislation before it can be published.

Rulesets and legal templates only

Planned
Auditor / Regulator
1 external

Client-money auditors and regulators need to inspect without being able to change anything.

Time-boxed, read-only, everything logged
