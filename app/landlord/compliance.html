Better than the law is always allowed. Less never is.

Every setting in openrent.me has a legal floor. You can move above it — a longer notice, a smaller deposit, a kinder exit clause — and your listing earns a “Better terms” badge. You cannot move below it, however the contract is worded.

How updates reach you

When the law changes we publish a new ruleset version and tell you which of your tenancies it touches: all tenancies immediately, new contracts only, or at the next renewal. Existing signed contracts are never rewritten silently.
