The questions people ask most. For anything else, message your manager in the app or call 800 RENT ME (800 736 863).

Renting

Is it really free to search and view?

Yes. You pay the AED 249 Move.me fee only when a tenancy is agreed. There is no commission.

What is Ejari and do I need it?

Ejari is the official registration of your tenancy with RERA. You need it to open a DEWA account, sponsor family visas and bring a case to the Rental Disputes Center. We register it for you and pass on the government fee at cost.

Can I pay monthly instead of with cheques?

Yes, where the landlord offers it. Under Dubai’s Flexi Rent initiative the annual rent is the same whether you pay monthly, quarterly or in one cheque; the plan is written into your contract.

How much can my rent go up at renewal?

Only as far as Decree 43 of 2013 allows, measured against the Smart Rental Index for your building, and only if your landlord tells you at least 90 days before the contract ends. We check both automatically.

Can I rent out a room to a friend?

Not on your own. Since the 2026 shared-housing law, letting rooms or bed spaces needs a permit held by the owner or a licensed operator. Family members and registered occupants are fine.

Letting

Can I really advertise without an agent?

Adverts in Dubai need a Trakheesi permit, which is issued through a licensed brokerage. openrent.me is that brokerage: you sign a Form A marketing agreement in the app and we obtain the permit — you stay in control of viewings, tenant choice and terms.

I need my property back. What do I do?

For your own use, a sale, or major works you must give 12 months’ notice at the end of a term, stating the reason, through a notary public or registered mail. The notices tool drafts and serves it correctly and tracks the date.

My tenant has not paid.

Follow the arrears path: reminder, then a 30-day notice through the notary, then — if unpaid — a complete case file for the Rental Disputes Center. Cutting services or changing locks is unlawful and is not something the platform will help with.

Who holds the deposit?

We do, in a safeguarded client account. It is released within 14 days of check-out, less any deduction supported by photo evidence from the condition reports.

The platform

What is a ruleset?

A numbered version of the legal rules the product enforces. When the law changes we publish a new one and tell you whether it affects existing tenancies, only new ones, or your next renewal.

Is my data safe?

Identity documents are verified through UAE Pass and never shown to the other party — they see a result. You can download or delete your data in line with UAE data-protection law.
