HOW IT WORKS The whole tenancy

From “to let”
to keys back.

Who does what at each stage — and what the platform takes off everyone’s plate.

1

Listing

Tenant

Browse adverts that all carry a permit number and a verified-owner badge.

Landlord

Upload the title deed, sign Form A, add photos, set rent and terms.

openrent.me

Matches owner to the DLD record, obtains the Trakheesi permit and QR, publishes.

DXB-002 · PermitDXB-003 · Owner check
2

Viewings and offers

Tenant

Book a slot, view, then offer a rent, payment plan and move-in date.

Landlord

Host or delegate viewings. Compare offers on verified results.

openrent.me

Pre-qualifies enquiries, hosts viewings on request, keeps the record.

3

Verification and contract

Tenant

Sign in with UAE Pass; consent to visa, income and credit checks. Sign the contract.

Landlord

Set clauses — each shows its legal minimum. Countersign.

openrent.me

Runs checks, builds the bilingual unified contract, blocks unlawful terms.

DXB-001 · Written & registeredOR-102 · Fair way out
4

Money and Ejari

Tenant

Pay the deposit and first instalment; set up direct debit or hand over cheques.

Landlord

Receive rent to your bank; cheques held in custody.

openrent.me

Safeguards the deposit, registers Ejari, issues receipts.

OR-101 · Deposit standardDXB-PAY · Payment terms
5

Move-in

Tenant

Walk through with the field agent; add notes within 7 days. Register who lives with you.

Landlord

Approve the condition report.

openrent.me

Photographs every room, records meter readings and keys, guides DEWA set-up.

DXB-OCC · Co-occupants
6

Living there

Tenant

Pay rent, report repairs with photos, message your landlord.

Landlord

Approve repairs within your spending limit; see rent arrive.

openrent.me

Decides who pays, dispatches vetted contractors, handles emergencies in 4 hours.

DXB-016 · RepairsOR-104 · EmergenciesDXB-034 · No cut-offs
7

Renewal

Tenant

Accept, counter or decline. Do nothing and the tenancy renews as is.

Landlord

Propose changes at least 90 days ahead, within the index cap.

openrent.me

Calculates the cap, enforces the deadline, renews Ejari.

DXB-014 · 90 daysDXB-009 · Rent capDXB-006 · Auto-renewal
8

Ending and settling

Tenant

Give notice, book check-out, get the deposit back in 14 days.

Landlord

Serve lawful notices only; evidence any deduction.

openrent.me

Runs the inspection, mediates, cancels Ejari, prepares the RDC file if needed.

DXB-025B · 12-month noticeDXB-021 · Wear and tearDXB-RDC · Disputes
