How it works in detail
Legal guardrails

Offer more than the law asks. Never less.

Dubai’s tenancy rules are precise: a 90-day window to change terms, rent rises tied to the Smart Rental Index, 12 months’ notarised notice to recover a home. openrent.me turns each rule into a guardrail, so the wrong thing simply cannot be sent.

  • Rent sliders stop at the legal maximum for that building
  • Notices are only offered for lawful reasons and periods
  • There is no button for cutting services or locking out
  • Better-than-law terms are highlighted for tenants as “Better”
