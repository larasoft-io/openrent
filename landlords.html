How letting works

Six steps. Most take minutes.

1

Prove it is yours

Upload the title deed and sign in with UAE Pass. We match it to the Dubai Land Department record. Acting for an owner abroad? Add your power of attorney.

2

Advertise with a permit

Sign Form A in the app. As a licensed brokerage we obtain the Trakheesi permit and QR code, so your advert is lawful from the first minute.

3

Viewings your way

Offer time slots and host them yourself, or send a field agent for AED 99. Enquiries arrive pre-qualified.

4

Choose on facts

Compare offers side by side: verified identity, visa, income ratio and credit band. You see results, never someone’s private documents.

5

Sign and register

The unified tenancy contract is built clause by clause with the legal minimum shown beside each one. Both sign with UAE Pass; we register Ejari.

6

Collect and look after

Direct debit, card or cheque custody. Repairs with spending limits you set. Renewals and notices on rails.

Guardrails that protect you

The mistakes that lose cases at the RDC — made impossible

  • Late rent increase? After the 90-day cut-off the option locks and we tell you why.
  • Increase too high? The slider stops at the Smart Rental Index maximum for your building.
  • Need the property back? Only lawful reasons are offered, with 12 months’ notarised notice.
  • Rent unpaid? A fixed path: reminder, notarised 30-day notice, complete RDC file.
  • Room-by-room letting? Blocked without a shared-housing permit under the 2026 law.
